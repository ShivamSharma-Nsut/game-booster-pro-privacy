Privacy Policy

Game Booster Pro · Last updated 4 October 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how the Game Booster Pro mobile application ("the App", "we", "us") handles information. Please read it before using the App.

In short: we do not collect, store, or transmit any personal data. The App has no accounts, no advertising, and no analytics. Everything it reads about your device stays on your device.

1. Information We Collect

We collect no personal information. The App does not ask you to create an account, does not request your name, email address, phone number, or location, and does not transmit any information about you to us or to any third party.

2. Information Processed On Your Device

To show you device statistics and to offer the boost features, the App reads the following directly from the operating system. This information is displayed on screen and is never uploaded, shared, or saved anywhere outside your device:

  • Device performance data — available and total RAM, free and total storage, battery level and battery temperature.
  • Installed game detection — the App checks whether a small, fixed list of supported games (for example PUBG Mobile, Free Fire, Call of Duty Mobile, BGMI, Valorant Mobile, Asphalt 9, Apex Legends Mobile) is installed, so it can show you which games it can boost. These package names are declared individually in the App's manifest. The App does not request the QUERY_ALL_PACKAGES permission and cannot see your full list of installed apps.
  • Network status and latency — the App checks whether you are online and measures round-trip latency by opening a brief connection to the public DNS server at 8.8.8.8. No data about you is sent in this check; it only measures how long the connection takes.

3. Information Stored On Your Device

The App saves a few small preferences locally, using your device's standard app storage, so that it remembers your choices between sessions:

  • The game you last selected.
  • Your GFX Tool settings, if you choose "Remember my settings".
  • A counter and timestamp used to decide when to show the Play Store review prompt.

This data never leaves your device and is deleted when you uninstall the App or clear its storage.

4. Permissions

  • Vibration — used only for haptic feedback on the boost slider and buttons.
  • Package visibility — used only to detect the specific supported games listed in Section 2.

The App requests no runtime permissions, and asks for no access to your contacts, camera, microphone, photos, files, SMS, call logs, or location.

5. Advertising and Analytics

The App contains no advertising and no analytics or tracking software. We do not use Google AdMob, Firebase Analytics, or any comparable advertising, attribution, or crash-reporting service. No advertising identifier is read or used.

6. Third-Party Services

The App does not send your data to any third party. Two features may hand you off to apps already on your device:

  • Google Play in-app review — the App may ask Google Play to show its standard rating dialog. Any rating or review you submit is handled by Google under Google's Privacy Policy, not by us.
  • Opening a game or store listing — tapping "Launch" or "Install" opens the game, or its Google Play listing, using your device's normal app-launch mechanism.

7. What The App Does Not Do

We want to be plain about the limits of a utility app like this one. Game Booster Pro cannot modify another app's internal graphics settings, cannot change system-wide device settings, and cannot override the performance limits set by your device's manufacturer. Its refresh-rate request applies only to its own window, using a public Android API. The boost and GFX sequences are presentational.

8. Children's Privacy

The App is not directed at children under the age of 13, and because it collects no personal information, it does not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided us with personal information, please contact us and we will address it.

9. Data Security

Because no personal information is collected or transmitted, there is no personal data on our side that could be exposed in a breach. Preferences stored on your device are protected by your device's own app-sandboxing and security features.

10. Your Rights

You can remove everything the App has stored at any time by clearing the App's data from your device settings, or by uninstalling the App. Since we hold no personal data about you, there is nothing for us to export, correct, or delete on your behalf.

11. Changes To This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. The revised version will be posted on this page with an updated "Last updated" date. Continued use of the App after a change means you accept the revised policy.
